# Hey, I'm Nagendra 👋

I'm an engineer who spends most of my working hours designing and shipping **AI-native distributed systems** — the kind that handle production-grade load, operate with autonomous agents, and need to remain secure even when things get adversarial.

## What I Work On

I'm focused on the intersection of:

- **Agentic Systems** — Multi-agent orchestration, tool-use, memory architectures, agent identity and authorisation
- **AI Infrastructure** — High-throughput inference pipelines, model serving tiers, KV-cache strategies, cost governance at scale
- **Cybersecurity** — Zero-trust for non-human identities, prompt injection defences, LLM guardrail architecture
- **System Design** — Event-driven architectures, distributed telemetry, real-time stream processing

## Why This Blog

Engineering in the AI space right now is genuinely hard in ways that aren't well-documented. The models improve weekly, the attack surface shifts accordingly, and the infrastructure patterns that worked six months ago may already be inadequate.

I write here to share what I've learned — the decisions I made, the ones I'd revisit, and the patterns that have proven durable under production conditions.

Posts are infrequent but substantive. No fluff, no hot takes for their own sake.

## Background

I've worked across infrastructure, platform, and product engineering roles. My current obsession is making agentic AI systems reliable, observable, and secure enough to trust with consequential tasks.
